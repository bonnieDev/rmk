How selection works. Every track declares a year window and, where it belongs to a people rather than a period, a home ground. Time decides what is possible; place decides which of the possible — at 800 CE the Rhine, Baghdad and the Orkhon each get a different track. A track that names a place and is playing somewhere else is wrong however strong its century, so a mismatch is penalised rather than merely unrewarded.

The bed. One track claims no moment in particular and plays only when nothing specific does — which is most of a 12,000-year timeline. Without it the score is silent between its islands, and silence reads as broken rather than as restraint.

The axis is non-linear, and has to be: Toba sits at −74,000 and the Abbasids at 800. On a linear axis everything after the Neolithic is one pixel wide.
