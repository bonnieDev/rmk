# Kinedic Bloom Use Policy

**Bloom talks to the person, never about them.**

Bloom notices friction (a shaking hand, a stall, a missed button) so the page can change *for the person using it*. Like a watch that tells you your balance is off, everything it notices is for that person. It is not information about them for anyone else.

## You may

- Adapt the page for the person using it: everything Bloom does on its own.
- Count friction **about your form**, never about who experienced it ("people miss the Submit button," "this question makes people stall"), in order to fix the form.
- Pass along a request the person **explicitly makes**, such as `bloom_assist=call`, to the people who will help them with it, and only for that purpose.

## You may not

Use Bloom, or anything it observes, to:

- identify, profile, score, rank or track individuals;
- infer, record or store anything about a person's health, disability or abilities;
- share anything Bloom observes with insurers, employers, lenders, landlords, data brokers, advertisers, or anyone deciding something about the person;
- make or influence decisions about eligibility, benefits, coverage, employment, pricing or credit;
- link friction data to a person's identity, account or application.

## What Bloom's signals are not

Bloom's signals are **not medical and not diagnostic**. They are rough hints about how a page is being used, and they are unreliable about any one person: a shaky tap might be a bus ride, and a stall might be a phone call. They must never be treated as evidence about anyone.
